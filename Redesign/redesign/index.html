01 / Aesthetic Mandate

Warm Civic Minimalist Philosophy

The Civic-Library Paradox

Traditional government portals and RSS readers suffer from dry, hostile, and uninviting layout systems. We aim to present federal agency statements with absolute institutional clarity, combined with the friendly atmosphere of a public library. Our system utilizes 'Liberty Blue' to anchor structural layouts, while softening the content stream with warm, legible serif typography and smart highlights.

Radical Progressive Disclosure

We never expect general consumers or researchers to read raw, unformatted 100-page federal registers immediately. Instead, we present agency releases in three progressively deeper states: a quick 10-second AI policy summary, a 2-minute narrative breakdown timeline, and a highly scannable link to the raw official agency dossier.
